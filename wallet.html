Wallet

A personal finance app that shows your balance, your transactions and where the money goes.

Overview

The wallet screen shows the total balance with Add, Pay and Send actions and switches between transactions and upcoming bills. The home page adds income, expenses and a transaction history. The statistics page charts spending by day, week, month or year and lists the top spending items. The profile page holds account information, the message center, security settings and privacy.

What is inside

  • Total balance with Add, Pay and Send actions
  • Transaction history with income and expenses
  • Statistics chart with day, week, month and year views
  • Top spending list
  • Profile with account info, message center, login and security, and data and privacy
  • Transaction detail screens for income and expenses
